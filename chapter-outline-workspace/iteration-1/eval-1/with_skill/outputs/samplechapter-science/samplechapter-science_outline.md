# Chapter 5: The Fundamental Unit of Life

## Chapter Description
This chapter explains what cells are, how they were discovered, and how they are organised inside. It covers the cell membrane, nucleus, organelles, and how cells divide — because every living thing is made of cells, and understanding them is the foundation of biology.
